Rulecard is installed

Capture an SAT or ACT question and get the answer plus the real rules behind it: SAT Reading & Writing and Math, ACT English, Math, Reading and Science.

First, pin it. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Rulecard.

  1. Open the question

    Open a SAT Suite Question Bank PDF, a practice-test question you're reviewing in My Practice, or an ACT practice test. Scroll so the whole question and its answer choices are on screen.

  2. Press Capture this tab

    Click the Rulecard icon to open the side panel, then press Capture this tab. That's the whole thing: it reads the question and explains it.

    The very first time, Chrome shows a box asking if Rulecard can see the tab you capture. Click Allow. You never have to do it again.

    PDF saved on your computer? Chrome also needs Allow access to file URLs turned on for Rulecard, once; the panel shows you exactly where. PDF open in Adobe Acrobat's viewer? Click the Rulecard icon while you're on it.

  3. Get the rules

    You get the answer and a few short bullets with the real rules behind it, in plain words. No long College Board rationale.

    Then tap the letter you picked to see exactly why it's wrong.

  4. Make it stick

    Today's plan sits at the bottom of Home (tap it to open). Missed rules come back for Review on a spaced schedule, and the toolbar badge shows how many are due. Export to Anki or print a study sheet any time.

Tip: in the side panel press ? for keyboard shortcuts: C to capture, 1–4 for the letter you picked, R to review.
