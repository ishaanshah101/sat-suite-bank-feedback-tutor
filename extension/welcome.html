Rulecard is installed

Capture an SAT question and get the answer plus the 2 or 3 rules behind it, in Reading & Writing and in Math.

First, pin it. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Rulecard.

  1. Open the question

    Open a SAT Suite Question Bank PDF, or a practice-test question you're reviewing in My Practice. Scroll so the whole question and its answer choices are on screen.

  2. Press Capture this tab

    Click the Rulecard icon to open the side panel, then press Capture this tab. With the panel open, clicking the icon (or pressing Alt+Shift+R) captures too. Rulecard takes one screenshot of that tab, only when you ask.

    PDF saved on your computer? Chrome asks you to turn on Allow access to file URLs for Rulecard once; the panel shows you where.

  3. Get the 2 or 3 rules

    You get the answer and 2 or 3 short bullets with the real rules behind it, in plain words. No long College Board rationale.

    Then tap the letter you picked to see exactly why it's wrong.

  4. Make it stick

    The Plan tab has today's checklist. Missed rules come back for Review on a spaced schedule, and the toolbar badge shows how many are due. Export to Anki or print a study sheet any time.

Tip: in the side panel press ? for keyboard shortcuts: C to capture, 1–4 for the letter you picked, R to review.
