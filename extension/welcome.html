Rulecard is installed

Learn the one rule behind every Digital SAT miss, in Reading & Writing and in Math.

First, pin it. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Rulecard.

  1. Work a question

    Open the SAT Suite Question Bank, answer a Reading & Writing or Math question, then reveal the answer. Grid-in Math questions work too: type what you got.

  2. Capture it

    Click the Rulecard icon, press Alt + Shift + R, or right-click the page, then Capture this question with Rulecard. On any other site, select a question, right-click and choose Explain this question with Rulecard, or paste it into the panel.

  3. Get the rule

    Tell Rulecard what you picked and why. You get the rule it tested, why your pick breaks it, and a one-line takeaway.

  4. Make it stick

    Missed rules come back for Review on a spaced schedule, and Today's plan tells you which skills to work next, with the exact Question Bank filters. The toolbar badge shows how many rules are due. Export to Anki or print a study sheet any time.

Tip: in the side panel press ? for keyboard shortcuts: 1–4 to pick, Enter to explain, T for Test me, R to review.
