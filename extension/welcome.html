Rulecard is installed

Learn the one rule behind every Digital SAT miss, in Reading & Writing and in Math.

First, pin it. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Rulecard.

  1. Export your questions

    Work Reading & Writing or Math questions in the SAT Suite Question Bank, then export them as a PDF. Include the answers and explanations if you can: Rulecard translates the official explanation into the rules you need.

  2. Open the PDF in Rulecard

    Click the Rulecard icon, then Open your Question Bank PDF (or drop the file on the panel). It is read on your computer. Pick a question by the ID printed at the top of its page; only that question is sent.

  3. Get the rules

    Tell Rulecard what you picked. If the PDF has the official explanation, you get it translated into the 1 to 3 rules you actually need. If it has no answer, Rulecard explains the question simply and gives you the rules.

  4. Make it stick

    Missed rules come back for Review on a spaced schedule, and Today's plan tells you which skills to work next, with the exact Question Bank filters. The toolbar badge shows how many rules are due. Export to Anki or print a study sheet any time.

Tip: in the side panel press ? for keyboard shortcuts: 1–4 to pick, Enter to explain, T for Test me, R to review.
